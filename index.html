AI Team Capability Assessment

One shared workshop board. Each team member rates every skill from 1 to 5, marks primary strengths, and records career interests. Use the definitions and observable examples below so that ratings reflect demonstrated capability rather than confidence or job title.

Shared 1–5 capability rubric

Choose the highest level you can support with a recent example. A level is about practical independence and outcome quality—not years of experience. If unsure, choose the lower level and discuss it during calibration.

1 · AwarenessCan explain the basic concepts and recognise when the skill is relevant. Needs close guidance to apply it.
2 · DevelopingCan complete a well-defined task using examples, documentation, or review from someone more experienced.
3 · IndependentCan apply the skill end-to-end on familiar work, make sound choices, and explain the result.
4 · AdvancedHandles ambiguous or complex work independently, improves approaches, and coaches others.
5 · Expert / leadsSets direction and standards, solves novel problems, and creates measurable impact across teams.
Calibration prompt: “What did you personally do, what was the outcome, and who could verify it?” Use the skill descriptions to make this evidence-based and avoid rating the same outcome twice under different skills.

Individual self-assessment
