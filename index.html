AI Team Capability Assessment

One shared workshop board. Each team member rates every skill from 1 to 5, marks primary strengths, and notes their individual interest areas.

Shared 1–5 capability rubric

Choose the highest level you can support with a recent example. A level is about practical independence, not years of experience.

1 = Awareness
2 = Developing
3 = Independent
4 = Advanced
5 = Expert / leads

Individual self-assessment

Expand “What this means” under any skill before rating it. Click a star again to clear a rating, and use the interest dropdown to note interest in each skill.

Team heatmaps

Average ratings provide a conversation starter, not a performance score. Blank cells mean “not rated”.

Save & share workshop data

Data is stored only in this browser. Export the JSON after the workshop and share it securely with the facilitation team.
